Diatech · Client-Requested Approach

Routing every channel through Shopify

Why this adds risk

In this version, Net32, Dentira, ZenOne and other platforms all route through Shopify to reach SAP. Each channel still needs its own connector — Net32 via Commercium, one per platform alongside it — and then a further connector, APPSeConnect, to carry everything from Shopify into SAP. Every order and every inventory update takes an extra hop — and Shopify becomes a relay for orders it wasn't built to handle.
